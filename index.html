Tianming Wei 韦天铭

I'm currently a first-year PhD student at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University, working in the Tsinghua Embodied AI Lab (TEA Lab) under the supervision of Prof. Huazhe Xu. I previously graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).

My research interest mainly focuses on robotic manipulation and Sim2Real, with particular emphasis on how to enhance the robustness and generalization ability of manipulation policies.
